About

I design quantum error-correcting codes that are high-rate, single-shot decodable and ready for real hardware.

PhD student at UMass Amherst (Manning CICS), advised by Stefan Krastanov; I also work with Owen Gwilliam. I build quantum LDPC codes from algebra, simulate them at circuit level, and lead the code library in QuantumClifford.jl.

Seeking research internships

Quantum error correction and fault-tolerant architectures, with flexible timing. Happy to chat: fmian@umass.edu
