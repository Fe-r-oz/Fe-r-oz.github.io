Research

I use homological and commutative algebra, group theory and expander graphs to build quantum LDPC codes, and I test them under circuit-level noise with Stim and modern decoders. I'm most interested in codes whose structure makes them both efficient and practical, from asymptotically good lifted product and quantum Tanner codes to high-rate qLDPC codes.
